# RARE ITEM SPAWNER MOD

A No Man's Sky mod that allows players to easily craft rare items from simple materials using the exosuit inventory crafting system.

## How It Works

### Step 1: Open Inventory
- Press **TAB** on your keyboard to open the exosuit inventory

### Step 2: Craft Nanotubes
- Find nanotubes in your inventory or craft them from basic materials
- These serve as the base item for accessing rare crafting

### Step 3: Open Crafting Menu
- Hover your mouse cursor over the nanotubes
- Hold down the **E** key
- The crafting popup window will open

### Step 4: Select Rare Item
- Browse the list of 26 rare craftable items
- Click any item you want
- It instantly appears in your inventory

## Installation

1. Navigate to your No Man's Sky installation folder
2. Go to `GAMEDATA/MODS/`
3. Copy the entire `RARE-ITEM-SPAWNER` folder into MODS
4. Launch No Man's Sky
